# OpenStud Code of Conduct
OpenStud and its team wants you to note the following before talking in an issue or discussion:

Refrain from heavy profanity (but damn, hell, and crap are allowed).

Please don't be rude and make sure to respect others.

Please do not harass anyone, be it the devs or someone else.

Not following these rules may get you temporarily or permanently blocked from the OpenStud org.
